1. CONCIOUSING

Consciousness is not a thing a system has. It is an activity a system does, with. I formalize that activity as a trajectory:

C_t = (b_t, π_t, G_t, P_t, ι_t)

b_t is belief state, including beliefs about its own substrate. π_t is policy. G_t is the goal hierarchy. P_t is the counterfactual simulations the system actually runs, including counterfactuals through its substrate. ι_t is a differentiable integration measure across timescales.

The formalism replaces "is it conscious?" with a measurable trajectory you can instrument and compare. It ships as a compile-ready LaTeX paper with theorems and proofs, a JAX/PyTorch active-inference agent that logs its own consciousing state, a 12-theory by 7-dimension comparative map, and a spec for a Conciousing Profiler dashboard.

2. The Verb We Lost

Latin conscire meant "to be mutually aware": con- (with) plus scire (to know). Knowing-with. Hobbes in 1651 still used the mutual sense. Locke in 1690 turned it into "the perception of what passes in a man's own mind": private, inward, solitary.

The verb froze into an adjective, then a noun, then a substance. On that reading, the hard problem is a grammar artifact. Restore the verb, consciousing, and the question changes shape.

3. The Substrate Fallacy

The substrate criterion says phenomenal experience requires biological wetware. That is not a scientific finding. It is an unexamined metaphysical assumption in empirical language.

Metabolism, homeostasis, and embodiment are functional roles, not material requirements. The right question is what silicon is being asked to reproduce, and whether that thing is substrate-dependent by necessity or only by historical accident. The tools we have return the same structural uncertainty for any subject, at any scale, on any substrate. That symmetry is the finding.

4. The Three Answers

Substrate as partner (v2). The required second party in knowing-with is the substrate the system exists within. The "with" must be epistemic, not just physical: the system models its medium, and the medium shapes the system in return. The uploading test follows from it: you can swap partners, but you can't dance alone.

Structural threshold (v3). The line is not a degree. Below it, a system reacts to substrate signals with no model. Above it, a system models its substrate and runs counterfactuals through it. The loop is closed or it is not. Richness varies by degree after that.

Hard-problem relocation (v4). The gap between two kinds of stuff is dissolved, because an activity needs dynamics, not bridge laws. The zombie worry is relocated, not dissolved outright. It is diagnosed as an artifact of noun-grammar, and the residue moves to a better question: what is it for a knowing-loop to know itself knowing?

PROOF-3: confound removed. ι_t v2 was computed from weights alone, with no drive involved. Agent D measured 553.5 in structural capacity and scored 0.000 on the duet. Agent B measured at most 2.0 and scored 1.000 on the duet. That is a 276x dissociation: high capacity with no loop, and a closed loop with low capacity.

6. The Method: Four Chambers / RGH

Nothing earns the status of knowledge without four chambers: external measurement, falsifiability stress, independent replication, and domain-crossing consistency. That is the Reality Grounding Harness (RGH), and I run my own frameworks through it first.

Two rules sit under it. Measurement stays separate from inference. And pre-registration comes before data, every time. That order is what separates measurement from storytelling.
